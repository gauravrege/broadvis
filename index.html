Ask on WhatsApp
How much does CCTV installation cost in Mumbai?

It depends on the number of cameras, camera type (HD or IP), how many days of recording you need and the cabling distance. We do a free site survey and give you a written, itemised quote before any work starts.

Can I watch my CCTV cameras on my phone?

Yes. Every system we install can be viewed live and played back on an Android or iPhone app, with motion alerts. We set the app up on your phone at handover.

Do you install CCTV and intercoms for housing societies?

Yes. We cover gates, parking, lifts, corridors and perimeters, install society intercoms for buildings with 10 to 500 flats, and provide documentation for the committee.

Will the cameras keep recording during a power cut?

Yes, when the system includes a UPS. We size the UPS so recording continues through power cuts and switches over without a break.

Do you offer AMC for CCTV, intercom and fire alarm systems?

Yes. Our AMC covers quarterly inspections, cleaning and calibration, firmware updates, genuine spare parts and 24/7 breakdown support.

Which areas do you serve?

We are based in Chembur and work across Mumbai and Navi Mumbai, including Ghatkopar, Kurla, Sion, Wadala, Govandi, Mankhurd, Vikhroli, Powai, Mulund, Vashi, Nerul, Belapur and Kharghar.
